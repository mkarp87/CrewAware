Computed, not stored

Unit contact state (normal, late, no contact) comes from the newest positions.received_at, the moving flag and site_rules.

Repeater status (online, degraded, offline) comes from the newest repeater_checkins.received_at.

Nearest units are ranked by distance to the incident, counting only units that are available and not in no contact.
