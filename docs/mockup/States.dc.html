Status flow and check-in rules
Dispatcher status and contact state are two separate things. A unit can be Dispatched and No contact at the same time, and the map shows both.
INCIDENT FLOW
Available
Suggested for new incidents
Dispatched
En route to an incident
On scene
At the incident location
Dispatch
Arrived
Cleared by command
Dispatch sets the status and creates the assignment in one step. Command confirms before it takes effect. Clearing is always manual, so a unit never frees itself and gets double-assigned.
MANUAL STATES, SET BY COMMAND FROM ANY STATE
Busy
Shown dimmed, can be overridden
Off duty
Gray on map, never suggested
Busy and Off duty are decided by command and do not come from the radio. Setting a unit to either one removes its incident assignment. Moving a unit back to Available is another manual step.
CHECK-IN RULES, SEPARATE FROM STATUS
Parked units, fallback check-in every 15 min
Normal · reported within 15 min
Late · one missed check-in, 15 to 30 min, amber
No contact · over 30 min, gray, not suggested
Units last seen moving, tighter limits
Normal · under 2 min
Late · silent for 2 to 10 min while moving, amber. Most likely lost coverage or a problem.
No contact · over 10 min, gray, not suggested
